How the Estimation Works

Our calculation formula is based on real commercial studio workflows, not theoretical AI generation speeds:

Hero Stills 1 hour of prompt generation and seed curation until reaching 1 winning frame, plus 5 to 8 hours of master high-end retouching in Photoshop and Magnific.
5-Second Motion Shots 1 hour of motion keyframe iteration and camera trajectory tests, plus 5 to 6 hours of motion deblurring, neural upscaling, and DaVinci color grading.
Turnaround Schedule Calculated against a focused 7-hour studio production workday, allowing realistic delivery timelines without burnout or rushed quality.
